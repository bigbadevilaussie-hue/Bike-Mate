BIKE-MATE V5.63 — VERO BOARD LAYOUT (A–T FULL MODULE)
====================================================

Rows A–T (20 strips ≈ 51 mm wide)
Length: 35 holes (≈ 89 mm)
Enclosure: open — choose any box that fits ~55 × 95 mm board

GOAL
----
All practical modules live ON the stripboard.
Only front-panel / external items use short flying leads.

ON-BOARD MODULES
  ESP32-C3 SuperMini
  MP1584EN (always-on 12→5 V)
  Mini 360 (switched 12→5 V)
  Dual USB-A charger module
  NEO-6M GPS
  IRF4905 + 2× 2N2222
  Voltage divider, filter, all resistors, C1–C4, TVS

OFF-BOARD (flying leads)
  OLED, NTC, wake button, alarm LED, buzzer, fan


PIN MAP (firmware unchanged)
----------------------------
GPIO 0  — Battery voltage divider → ADC  (+ 100 nF)
GPIO 1  — IRF4905 driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 2  — GPS RX (from GPS TX) + 10 kΩ pull-up to 3V3
GPIO 3  — NTC
GPIO 4  — Buzzer driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 5  — Wake button
GPIO 6  — OLED SDA
GPIO 9  — OLED SCL
GPIO 10 — Alarm LED


RAILS
-----
Row A  = +12 V always-on rail
Row T  = GND rail
Row B  = switched 12 V bus (after IRF4905)


ZONE MAP
--------
Cols  1– 8   Power input + MP1584 + divider
Cols  9–18   ESP32-C3 SuperMini + local decoupling
Cols 19–27   IRF4905 drivers + switched 12 V
Cols 28–35   Mini 360 + USB charger + GPS

============================================================
BUILD ORDER
============================================================

1. Trim board to 20 strips × 35 holes (A–T).
2. Make all cuts; continuity-check every one.
3. Fit female headers for SuperMini (cols 11 & 17, rows D–K).
4. Fit all discrete resistors, capacitors, TVS, IRF4905, 2N2222s.
5. Fit the five (or more) jumpers.
6. Mount MP1584EN, Mini 360, USB charger, GPS modules (use pin headers or direct solder).
7. Continuity check: no short between A and T, no unintended links.
8. Power up without SuperMini: confirm 5 V from MP1584 and from Mini 360 (when driven).
9. Insert SuperMini, measure sleep current (ACC off).
10. Wire the remaining flying leads (OLED, NTC, button, LED, buzzer, fan).


============================================================
KNOWN ISSUES / NOTES
============================================================

- Board is ~51 mm wide. Previous 40 mm enclosure will not fit — choose a larger box.
- GPS antenna should face an open face or the top of the enclosure.
- C3 electrolytic is the tallest part on the left; check lid clearance.
- GPIO 2 10 kΩ pull-up leak into unpowered GPS still applies — measure sleep current.
- GPIO 9 (BOOT) must be held high by OLED I²C pull-ups at reset.
- No reverse-polarity protection beyond the polarised SAE connector.
- SuperMini power LED / LDO may dominate sleep current — measure and desolder if needed.
- Firmware pin map and software are unchanged from the previous 13-strip version.
