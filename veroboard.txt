BIKE-MATE V5.63 — VERO BOARD LAYOUT
====================================
40 × 40 × 100 mm enclosure
Vero board trimmed to fit inside

PIN MAP
-------
GPIO 0  — Battery voltage divider → ADC
GPIO 1  — IRF4905 driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 2  — GPS TX + 10kΩ pull-up to 3V3
GPIO 3  — NTC + 10kΩ to GND
GPIO 4  — Buzzer driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 5  — Wake button → GND (internal pull-up)
GPIO 6  — OLED SDA
GPIO 9  — OLED SCL
GPIO 10 — Alarm red LED (via 330Ω)
3V3     — OLED VCC, GPS pull-up, NTC top
5V      — ESP32 power from MP1584EN

IRF4905 DRIVER
--------------

12V (source) ── 10kΩ ── IRF4905 GATE
                            │
                       2N2222 COLLECTOR
                            │
                       EMITTER ── GND
                       BASE ── 1kΩ ── GPIO 1
                       BASE ── 10kΩ ── GND


BUZZER CIRCUIT
--------------

12V ── SFM-27 red (+)
            │
       SFM-27 black (−)
            │
       2N2222 COLLECTOR
            │
       EMITTER ── GND
       BASE ── 1kΩ ── GPIO 4
       BASE ── 10kΩ ── GND


LEDs
----
GPIO 10 ── 330Ω ── Red LED (alarm) ── GND


FAN
---
Mini 360 5V ──┬── Fan red
              │
              ├── 1N4148 cathode
              │
              └── Fan black ──┬── 1N4148 anode
                              │
                              └── GND

Place the diode at the fan's terminals, not on the
vero, to keep the flyback path short.


GPS
---
Mini 360 5V ── GPS VCC
GND ────────── GPS GND
GPS TX ─────── GPIO 2
GPS RX ─────── (not connected)


OLED (PANEL ONLY — off-board)
-----------------------------
Breakout board inside box, wired to ESP32:
  VCC ── 3V3
  GND ── GND
  SDA ── GPIO 6
  SCL ── GPIO 9

Check the breakout for I2C pull-ups (4.7kΩ on SDA/SCL
to 3V3). Add if missing.

============================================================
BEFORE POWER-UP
============================================================

1. Verify MP1584EN output is 5.0 V (multimeter, no load).
2. Verify Mini 360 output is 5.0 V (multimeter, no load).
3. Check OLED FFC pitch matches breakout socket.
4. Check OLED breakout has I2C pull-ups. Add if missing.
5. Confirm IRF4905 drain does NOT connect to 12V (that's source).
6. For SA16CA bidirectional there is no banded end — either leg
   to 12V, either leg to GND.
7. Check fan flyback diode orientation: cathode to fan (+), anode
   to fan (−).


============================================================
POWER BUDGET
============================================================

Always-on rail (kill switch ON, accState OFF):
  MP1584EN quiescent      ~100 µA
  Voltage divider         ~116 µA
  ESP32 light sleep       ~30 µA
  ────────────────────────────────
  Total                   ~250 µA

  On 10.5 Ah AGM battery: ~2.6 % per month.

Switched rail (accState ON):
  Mini 360 → GPS          45 mA
  Mini 360 → Fan          150 mA
  USB charger             up to 2 A (phone)
  ────────────────────────────────
  Total                   up to ~2.2 A


============================================================
KNOWN ISSUES / TODO
============================================================

- ACC indicator LED removed. GPIO 1 now drives IRF4905 only.
  If a visual ACC indicator is wanted, use a spare GPIO
  (e.g. GPIO 11) with a 330Ω resistor + green LED.
- Fan is external to the 40mm face. If inside, box ID must
  be at least 42mm.
- GPS cold-start takes 30–60 s. Ride start may log before
  a fix is acquired.
- NTC B value in firmware is 4600, physical part is B=3950.
  Temperature readings are ~1.5 °C off.
- Firmware bit-bangs GPIO 4 at 2.8 kHz. SFM-27 is
  self-oscillating; the bit-bang just gates it.
