BIKE-MATE V5.63 — VERO BOARD LAYOUT
====================================

40 × 40 × 100 mm enclosure
Vero board trimmed to fit inside

(Revised: fuse 1A → 3A, added GPIO 0 filter cap, corrected power budget,
 added strapping-pin / GPS-leak / reverse-polarity notes. See KNOWN ISSUES.)


PIN MAP
-------

GPIO 0  — Battery voltage divider → ADC  (100 nF to GND at the pin)
GPIO 1  — IRF4905 driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 2  — GPS RX (from GPS TX) + 10kΩ pull-up to 3V3   (strapping pin, must be HIGH at boot)
GPIO 3  — NTC + 10kΩ to GND
GPIO 4  — Buzzer driver (2N2222 base)
GPIO 5  — Wake button → GND (internal pull-up)
GPIO 6  — OLED SDA
GPIO 9  — OLED SCL                       (strapping/BOOT pin, must be HIGH at reset)
GPIO 10 — Alarm red LED (via 330Ω)

3V3 — OLED VCC, GPS pull-up, NTC top
5V  — ESP32 power from MP1584EN

IRF4905 DRIVER
--------------

12V (source) ── 10kΩ ── IRF4905 GATE
                              │
                        2N2222 COLLECTOR
                              │
                        EMITTER ── GND

BASE ── 1kΩ  ── GPIO 1
BASE ── 10kΩ ── GND


BUZZER CIRCUIT
--------------

12V ── SFM-27 red (+)
            │
       SFM-27 black (−)
            │
       2N2222 COLLECTOR
            │
       EMITTER ── GND

BASE ── 1kΩ  ── GPIO 4
BASE ── 10kΩ ── GND


LEDs
----

GPIO 10 ── 330Ω ── Red LED (alarm) ── GND


FAN
---

Mini 360 5V ──┬── Fan red
              │
              ├── 1N4148 cathode
              │
              └── Fan black ──┬── 1N4148 anode
                              │
                              └── GND

Place the diode at the fan's terminals, not on the
vero, to keep the flyback path short.


GPS
---

Mini 360 5V ── GPS VCC
GND ────────── GPS GND
GPS TX ─────── GPIO 2   (10kΩ pull-up to 3V3 — see KNOWN ISSUES)
GPS RX ─────── (not connected)


OLED (PANEL ONLY — off-board)
-----------------------------

Breakout board inside box, wired to ESP32:

VCC ── 3V3
GND ── GND
SDA ── GPIO 6
SCL ── GPIO 9

Check the breakout for I2C pull-ups (4.7kΩ on SDA/SCL
to 3V3). Add if missing. GPIO 9 is the BOOT strapping pin:
the pull-up must hold it HIGH at reset or the chip will enter
download mode.

============================================================
BEFORE POWER-UP
============================================================

1. Verify MP1584EN output is 5.0 V (multimeter, no load).
2. Verify Mini 360 output is 5.0 V (multimeter, no load).
3. Check OLED FFC pitch matches breakout socket.
4. Check OLED breakout has I2C pull-ups. Add if missing.
5. Confirm IRF4905 drain does NOT connect to 12V (that's source).
6. For SA16CA bidirectional there is no banded end — either leg
   to 12V, either leg to GND.
7. Check fan flyback diode orientation: cathode to fan (+), anode
   to fan (−).
8. Check the divider node (GPIO 0) reads about 1.3 V at 14 V in,
   with the 100 nF cap fitted.
9. With ACC off and GPS unpowered, measure total sleep current
   from the battery (see POWER BUDGET and KNOWN ISSUES).


============================================================
POWER BUDGET
============================================================

Always-on rail (kill switch ON, accState OFF):

  MP1584EN quiescent     ~100 µA   (measure the real module)
  Voltage divider        ~116 µA   (12.6 V / 108.78 kΩ)
  ESP32 light sleep       ~30 µA   (measure: SuperMini power LED and
                                    onboard LDO can add far more)
  ────────────────────────────────
  Total                  ~250 µA

  On 10.5 Ah AGM battery: 250 µA × 720 h = 180 mAh ≈ 1.7 % per month.
  (If your measured total is nearer 380 µA, that is ≈ 2.6 % per month.)

Switched rail (accState ON), current drawn from the 12 V side:

  Mini 360 → GPS 45 mA @ 5V, Fan 150 mA @ 5V   ≈ 0.1 A @ 12V
  USB charger, 2 A @ 5V out (≈10 W, ~85 % eff.) ≈ 1.0 A @ 12V
  ────────────────────────────────
  Total up to                                   ≈ 1.1 A @ 12V

  This is why the inline fuse is 3A, not 1A: a 1A slow-blow
  fuse would nuisance-blow during a full-rate phone charge.
  Wire gauge from fuse to vero must be rated for the fuse.


============================================================
KNOWN ISSUES / TODO
============================================================

- ACC indicator LED removed. GPIO 1 now drives IRF4905 only.
  If a visual ACC indicator is wanted, use a spare GPIO
  (e.g. GPIO 11) with a 330Ω resistor + green LED.

- Fan is external to the 40mm face. If inside, box ID must
  be at least 42mm.

- GPS cold-start takes 30–60 s. Ride start may log before
  a fix is acquired.

- NTC B value in firmware is 4600, physical part is B=3950.
  Temperature readings are ~1.5 °C off. Fix: set B to 3950
  in Config.h and verify against a reference thermometer.

- Firmware bit-bangs GPIO 4 at 2.8 kHz. SFM-27 is
  self-oscillating; the bit-bang just gates it.

- GPS TX pull-up leak (to verify). The 10kΩ pull-up from 3V3
  on GPIO 2 may push 0.1–0.3 mA into the GPS TX pin's protection
  diodes while the GPS is unpowered (ACC off). That is as large as
  the whole sleep budget. Measure sleep current with ACC off; if
  high, try a series resistor between GPS TX and GPIO 2, or another
  way to hold GPIO 2 high at boot. GPIO 2 is a strapping pin and
  must be HIGH at reset.

- GPIO 9 (OLED SCL) is the BOOT strapping pin. I2C pull-ups must
  hold it HIGH at reset. If the breakout pulls it LOW, the chip
  enters download mode.

- No reverse-polarity protection. The bidirectional SA16CA does
  not block a reversed battery, and the IRF4905 body diode passes
  it to the switched rail. The polarised SAE plug makes this
  unlikely; a series Schottky or P-MOSFET on the input would close
  the gap.

- SuperMini power LED / LDO. Many SuperMini boards have an
  always-on red power LED and a weak regulator. Check yours; if
  sleep current is high, desolder the LED.

- ADC source impedance is ~9 kΩ (98.8k || 9.98k). The 100 nF cap
  on GPIO 0 steadies readings. Re-check voltage calibration after
  fitting it.

- Heat and water.

- Recommended extra capacitors (not on the original layout):
  - 100 nF ceramic across SuperMini 5V / GND at the header
  - 10–47 µF bulk on the same 5V rail near the SuperMini
  - Optional 100 nF on the switched 12V rail near the IRF4905
 A 2 A USB charger plus two bucks and a GPS in
  a 40×40×100 mm box will run warm. Side vents need water
  protection (baffle, mesh, or a drain path) for motorcycle use.


============================================================
STRIPBOARD LAYOUT (13 strips A-M x 30 holes, about 33 x 76 mm)
============================================================

Picture: bike_mate_veroboard_layout.png (same folder).
Untested design: check every cut and connection with a continuity
tester before powering.

Strips run left to right. Row A = +12V rail, row M = GND rail.
Holes are referenced as ROW+COLUMN, e.g. D5 = row D, column 5.

ESP32-C3 SuperMini sits on female headers at columns 11 and 17,
rows D-K, USB-C end toward the GND rail.

  Row   Left header (col 11)   Right header (col 17)
  ---   --------------------   ---------------------
  D     GPIO 0                 GPIO 21 (unused)
  E     GPIO 1                 GPIO 20 (unused)
  F     GPIO 2                 GPIO 10 (alarm LED)
  G     GPIO 3                 GPIO 9  (OLED SCL)
  H     GPIO 4                 GPIO 8  (unused)
  I     3V3                    GPIO 7  (unused)
  J     GND                    GPIO 6  (OLED SDA)
  K     5V                     GPIO 5  (wake button)

Verify pin order against your board's silkscreen. Header rows
assumed 6 holes apart; if yours are 7, shift everything right of
the module by one column and move the first cut to the midpoint.

STRIP CUTS (17) - remove the copper at the hole
-----------------------------------------------
Column 14, rows D to K   (8 cuts)  split left/right header groups
Column 22, rows D to K   (8 cuts)  split pad area from driver zone
I18                      (1 cut)   isolate unused GPIO 7 pin

BUILD ORDER
-----------
1. Trim board to 13 strips x 30 holes.
2. Make the 17 cuts; check each with continuity.
3. Fit female headers at columns 11 and 17 (rows D-K).
4. Fit resistors, C1 and D1 (R6, R8, R10 upright).
5. Fit U1, Q1, Q2.
6. Add the five jumpers.
7. Wire the pads.
8. Continuity check: no short between rail A and rail M, no
   unintended links between rows.
9. Power up without the SuperMini: confirm 5V, 3V3 and GND at its
   sockets.
10. Insert the SuperMini, check voltage reading and sleep current.
